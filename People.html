People

As with life, this research project wouldn't be possible without the support and collaboration of its own Very Important People and Organisations

Dalia Avello Vega - Lead Researcher



Dalia is the principal investigator and enthusiastic leader of the
My VIP Scotland project. Trained as a psychologist, she has
dedicated most of her career to exploring the intersection
between adversity and children’s mental health in public policy
contexts. ​ For My VIP Scotland she is using longitudinal data to
learn about the influence that significant adults and peers have
in children’s lives as they grow.

Michael Heffernan - Young Persons Advisory Group Leader



Michael is an youth participation advisor to the My VIP
Scotland project. He is a Social Policy and Politics student
at the University of Edinburgh. Alongside his studies,
Michael is a longstanding ‘expert by experience’ and
brought his lived experience of human rights breaches
to projects at the Scottish Government and charitable
foundations trying to tackle these issues. For the project,
Michael has been working with Dalia, using his experience
of participation projects to construct an authentic and
empowering participation experience for the Young
Advisory Group.

Dotmaster



Dotmaster is an internationally renowned street artist and multi-media creator, with an eclectic career spanning more than three decades of practice. ​ Dotmaster's Rude Kids' stencils from Jopps Lane and the E&Ms building in Aberdeen (Nuart Aberdeen 2019) became the graphic inspiration for the project.
​ All the fun kids found on this website courtesy of Dotmaster. ​ ​

Mano Mandal



Mano is a researcher, photographer, and documentary film maker based in Edinburgh. They are currently writing up their PhD thesis in Social Anthropology. Adopting ethnographic and visual methods, their research explores themes of climate change, displacement, citizenship, and energy access.
​ All the photography used for the project courtesy of Mano.

Lorena Avello



Lore is a visual storytelling expert and strategic communications specialist.​ She transforms complex ideas into clear, impactful narratives. Lore specialises in executive presentations and has an extensive background in the pharmaceutical industry. By merging design and strategy, she improves communication, engagement, and decision-making.
​​ The creative direction and storytelling for this website courtesy of Lore.​
